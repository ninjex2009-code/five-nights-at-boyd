# Five Nights at Boyd

A Five Nights at Freddy's fan game set at McKinney Boyd High School, made in
GDevelop 5. Play it in a browser — no install.

Watch the halls from room 539 between 12 AM and 6 AM. Two doors, two hall
lights, one breaker.

**Controls** — push the mouse to a screen edge to turn your head. Upper button
each side is the door, lower one is the hall light. The bar along the bottom
raises the camera monitor.

- **Mr Biles** and the **Bearded Dragon** come from your left.
- **Mrs Andres** and **Mrs Chambers** come from your right. Mrs Chambers gives
  you about five seconds.
- Watching CAM 1C is the only thing that slows the Bearded Dragon down.
- Power drains faster with every door and light you hold. You have room for
  roughly one door shut all night, not two.

**Saturday School** is the hard fixed night. **Flextime** lets you set each
character from 0 to 20 yourself.

Made with GDevelop. Original Five Nights at Freddy's created by Scott Cawthon.
